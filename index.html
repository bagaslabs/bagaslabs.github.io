04 / ABOUT & EXPERIENCE

Curious about how things work.

I’m an Electrical Engineering student at the University of Jember, interested in the relationship between software and the physical world. That means exploring systems from more than one angle: writing code, understanding how data moves, and experimenting with devices that can sense or control their environment.

My interests include Lua game-bot experiments, reverse engineering, Go and C++ development, and IoT automation with platforms such as Raspberry Pi and Arduino. I enjoy learning by taking systems apart, asking better questions, and building small prototypes to test an idea.
